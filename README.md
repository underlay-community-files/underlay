# Underlay

**Free live backdrops behind your TradingView candles.** Animated scenes, 4K pictures, your own videos, a few tiny cats, and a Community shelf of designs shared by other traders.

Free forever. No account, no watermark, no paywall.

## Install (Chrome, Edge, Brave, Opera GX, Arc)

1. Go to **[Releases](../../releases/latest)** and download `Underlay-x.y.z.zip`.
2. Unzip it. You get a folder called **Underlay**. Move it somewhere it can stay, e.g. `Documents\Underlay`.
3. Open `chrome://extensions` (Edge: `edge://extensions`).
4. Turn on **Developer mode** (top right).
5. Click **Load unpacked** and choose the **Underlay** folder.
6. Click the puzzle piece 🧩 in the toolbar and **pin** Underlay.
7. Open any chart on [tradingview.com](https://www.tradingview.com/chart/) and click the Underlay icon to pick a backdrop.

Keep the folder where it is, because the browser runs Underlay from it.

## Updating

Download the new zip, unzip it over your **Underlay** folder (replace the files), then open Underlay and click **Finish update**.

## What's inside

- **Backdrops:** 49 drawn live scenes and pictures, plus 13 calm anime-style videos. Each shows its resolution and frame rate.
- **My media:** put your own videos, GIFs or pictures behind your chart. They stay on your computer.
- **Community:** add designs other traders shared, or share your own. Uploads are public and go live right away. A design is hidden after 3 reports.
- **Pets:** 8 little cats that walk along the bottom of your chart.
- **Look controls:** opacity, dim, blur, speed, colour, vignette, quality up to 4K, auto-rotate, and matching candle colours.
- `Alt+Shift+U` turns it on and off.

## Good to know

- Works on tradingview.com in the browser. It doesn't work in the TradingView desktop app.
- Some prop firms don't allow browser extensions on evaluation accounts. Check yours.
- If you remove Underlay while it's on, reset your chart colours in TradingView → Chart settings → Canvas.
- Not affiliated with or endorsed by TradingView.
